Biology Practical: Human Heart & Circulation

Visualise the four chambers and follow a simplified blood-flow pathway.

INTERACTIVE 3D MODEL
Drag to rotate · Scroll to zoom

3D model is a learning visualisation, not a medical or diagnostic model.

Principle / Formula

Right heart → lungs → left heart → body → right heart
Heart rate = beats per minute (BPM).

Experiment Controls

Working Process

  1. Identify right atrium, right ventricle, left atrium and left ventricle.
  2. Follow the blue pathway from the body to the lungs.
  3. Follow the red pathway from the lungs to the body.
  4. Change the heartbeat speed and observe the pulse animation.
  5. Write the blood-flow sequence and label the four chambers.

Observation

Adjust the controls and observe the model.
