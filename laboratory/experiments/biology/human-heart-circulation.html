Label key

Right atriumReceives deoxygenated blood from the body
Right ventriclePumps blood to lungs through pulmonary artery
Left atriumReceives oxygenated blood from lungs
Left ventriclePumps blood to body through aorta
ValvesHelp prevent backflow
KEY RELATIONSeptum|Separates right and left sides

Accuracy note Body → venae cavae → right atrium → right ventricle → lungs → left atrium → left ventricle → aorta → body

Principle / Formula

Right heart → lungs → left heart → body → right heart

Experiment Controls

Working Process

  1. Identify right atrium, right ventricle, left atrium and left ventricle.
  2. Follow the blue pathway from the body to the lungs.
  3. Follow the red pathway from the lungs to the body.
  4. Change the heartbeat speed and observe the pulse animation.
  5. Write the blood-flow sequence and label the four chambers.

Observation

Adjust the controls and observe the model.
