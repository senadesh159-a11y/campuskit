Biology Practical: Photosynthesis

Explore how light intensity and carbon dioxide availability affect a simplified photosynthesis model.

INTERACTIVE 3D MODEL
Drag to rotate · Scroll to zoom

3D model is a learning visualisation, not a medical or diagnostic model.

Principle / Formula

6CO₂ + 6H₂O — light → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂
Rate is influenced by light, CO₂ and temperature.

Experiment Controls

Working Process

  1. Observe the leaf, stem and sunlight in the model.
  2. Increase or decrease the light intensity slider.
  3. Change the carbon dioxide availability.
  4. Compare the illustrative photosynthesis-rate indicator.
  5. Record that another limiting factor, such as temperature, can also affect the rate.

Observation

Adjust the controls and observe the model.
