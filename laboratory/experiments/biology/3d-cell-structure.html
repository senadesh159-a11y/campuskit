NucleusContains most of the cell's genetic material
NucleolusInvolved in ribosome subunit production
MitochondriaMajor site of aerobic ATP production
Endoplasmic reticulumSynthesis and transport of proteins/lipids
Golgi apparatusModifies and packages cell products

Experiment Controls

Working Process

  1. Observe the cell membrane as the outer boundary.
  2. Rotate the model to locate the nucleus and nucleolus.
  3. Identify mitochondria, endoplasmic reticulum and Golgi body.
  4. Adjust cell scale and nucleus size to compare structures.
  5. Record the organelles and their main functions in your practical notebook.

Observation

Adjust the controls and observe the model.
