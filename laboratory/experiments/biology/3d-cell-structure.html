Biology Practical: 3D Cell Structure

Explore a simplified animal cell and its major organelles.

INTERACTIVE 3D MODEL
Drag to rotate · Scroll to zoom

3D model is a learning visualisation, not a medical or diagnostic model.

Principle / Formula

Cell = plasma membrane + cytoplasm + organelles
Nucleus stores genetic information; mitochondria release usable energy.

Experiment Controls

Working Process

  1. Observe the cell membrane as the outer boundary.
  2. Rotate the model to locate the nucleus and nucleolus.
  3. Identify mitochondria, endoplasmic reticulum and Golgi body.
  4. Adjust cell scale and nucleus size to compare structures.
  5. Record the organelles and their main functions in your practical notebook.

Observation

Adjust the controls and observe the model.
