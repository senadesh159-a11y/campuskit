Anthropology Practical: Human Skull & Craniometry

Study a simplified 3D skull model and learn basic cranial measurement concepts.

INTERACTIVE 3D MODEL
Drag to rotate · Scroll/pinch to zoom · Right-drag to pan

3D model is a learning visualisation, not a medical or diagnostic model.

Principle / Formula

Cephalic index = (Maximum head breadth ÷ Maximum head length) × 100
Measurements must follow a defined anthropometric protocol.

Experiment Controls

Working Process

  1. Rotate the skull and identify the cranium and facial region.
  2. Locate the eye sockets, nasal opening and jaw in the model.
  3. Change the viewing angle between front, side and three-quarter views.
  4. Discuss how anthropologists use standardised landmarks and instruments.
  5. Record that real craniometric measurements require a specimen and approved protocol.

Observation

Adjust the controls and observe the model.
