Experiment Controls

Working Process

  1. Observe the skull and vertebral column in the centre.
  2. Identify ribs and sternum as parts of the thoracic cage.
  3. Switch highlight between axial, upper-limb and lower-limb groups.
  4. Rotate the model to inspect the bones from different directions.
  5. Record the main bones and their broad functions.

Observation

Adjust the controls and observe the model.
