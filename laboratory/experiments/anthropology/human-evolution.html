Working Process

  1. Select a specimen group from the timeline slider.
  2. Observe changes in the simplified cranial shape.
  3. Adjust the braincase scale to understand relative proportions.
  4. Compare brow region, face projection and cranial vault.
  5. Note that real human evolution is branching and fossil interpretations use multiple kinds of evidence.
