Redox Titration

Estimation of Ferrous Ammonium Sulphate using standard potassium permanganate solution.

1. Aim

To determine the concentration of the given Ferrous Ammonium Sulphate (FAS) solution by titrating it against standard potassium permanganate (KMnO₄) solution in an acidic medium.

2. Apparatus Required

  • Burette and burette stand
  • Volumetric pipette
  • Conical flask
  • Beaker and funnel
  • Measuring cylinder
  • White tile

3. Chemicals Required

  • Standard potassium permanganate (KMnO₄) solution
  • Ferrous Ammonium Sulphate (FAS) solution
  • Dilute sulphuric acid (H₂SO₄)
  • Distilled water

4. Theory

Redox titration is based on an oxidation-reduction reaction in which electrons are transferred between two reacting substances.

In this experiment, ferrous ions (Fe²⁺) are oxidised to ferric ions (Fe³⁺), while permanganate ions (MnO₄⁻) are reduced to manganese ions (Mn²⁺) in an acidic medium.

MnO₄⁻ + 5Fe²⁺ + 8H⁺
→ Mn²⁺ + 5Fe³⁺ + 4H₂O

KMnO₄ acts as a self-indicator. The endpoint is indicated by the appearance of a faint permanent pink colour.

5. Formula

N₁V₁ = N₂V₂
  • N₁ = Normality of KMnO₄
  • V₁ = Volume of KMnO₄ used
  • N₂ = Normality of FAS
  • V₂ = Volume of FAS taken

Therefore:

N(FAS) = [N(KMnO₄) × V(KMnO₄)] / V(FAS)

6. Procedure

  1. Wash the burette and rinse it with KMnO₄ solution.
  2. Fill the burette with standard KMnO₄ solution and note its initial reading.
  3. Rinse the pipette with FAS solution.
  4. Transfer 20 ml of FAS solution into a clean conical flask.
  5. Add about 10 ml of dilute sulphuric acid.
  6. Titrate with KMnO₄ while continuously swirling the flask.
  7. Add KMnO₄ dropwise near the endpoint.
  8. Stop when a faint pink colour persists for about 30 seconds.
  9. Record the final burette reading and repeat for concordant readings.
The reaction mixture may be kept at room temperature for this FAS titration. Do not heat it unless your laboratory procedure specifically instructs you to.

8. Calculation

Suppose the following values are used:

  • Normality of KMnO₄ = 0.1 N
  • Volume of KMnO₄ = 19.77 ml
  • Volume of FAS = 20 ml
N₁V₁ = N₂V₂

0.1 × 19.77 = N₂ × 20

N₂ = 0.09885 N

The illustrative normality of FAS is 0.09885 N.

9. Result

The normality of the given Ferrous Ammonium Sulphate solution is determined by titration against standard potassium permanganate solution in an acidic medium.

Write the final numerical result using your actual concordant readings.

10. Precautions

  • Use only dilute sulphuric acid to provide the acidic medium.
  • Do not use hydrochloric acid or nitric acid in this titration.
  • Ensure there are no air bubbles in the burette tip.
  • Read the burette at eye level.
  • Add KMnO₄ dropwise near the endpoint.
  • Use clean apparatus and avoid contamination.
  • Wear safety goggles and gloves.
