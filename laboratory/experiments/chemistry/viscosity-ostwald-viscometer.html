Determination of Viscosity

Determination of the coefficient of viscosity of a given liquid using an Ostwald viscometer.

1. Aim

To determine the coefficient of viscosity of the given liquid using an Ostwald viscometer by comparing its flow time with that of water at the same temperature.

2. Apparatus Required

  • Ostwald viscometer
  • Stopwatch
  • Thermometer
  • Beaker
  • Measuring pipette
  • Rubber suction bulb
  • Water bath

3. Chemicals Required

  • Distilled water
  • Given liquid or glycerol solution

4. Theory

Viscosity is the property of a fluid by which it resists the relative motion between its different layers. It is also known as the internal friction of a liquid.

An Ostwald viscometer is used to determine the viscosity of a liquid by measuring the time taken by a fixed volume of liquid to flow through a narrow capillary tube.

The viscosity of a liquid depends on its density and the time required to flow through the capillary.

5. Formula

η₁ / η₂ = (ρ₁ × t₁) / (ρ₂ × t₂)

Where:

  • η₁ = Viscosity of the given liquid
  • η₂ = Viscosity of water
  • ρ₁ = Density of the given liquid
  • ρ₂ = Density of water
  • t₁ = Flow time of the given liquid
  • t₂ = Flow time of water
η₁ = η₂ × (ρ₁t₁ / ρ₂t₂)

The viscosity of water (η₂) and densities must be taken at the experimental temperature.

6. Procedure

  1. Clean the Ostwald viscometer properly and dry it.
  2. Fill the viscometer with a measured volume of distilled water.
  3. Place the viscometer vertically in a constant-temperature water bath.
  4. Allow the liquid to reach the bath temperature.
  5. Draw the liquid above the upper mark using a suction bulb.
  6. Allow it to flow freely and record the time taken by the meniscus to pass between the two marks.
  7. Repeat the measurement to obtain consistent readings.
  8. Repeat the same procedure using the given liquid.
  9. Record the densities and calculate the viscosity using the formula.
Use the same viscometer and maintain the same temperature for water and the given liquid.

  • Density of given liquid = 1.10 g/cm³

Write the final value with the correct unit after using your experimental readings.

10. Precautions

  • The viscometer must be clean and dry.
  • Keep the viscometer vertical during the experiment.
  • Maintain constant temperature throughout the experiment.
  • Avoid air bubbles inside the capillary tube.
  • Start and stop the stopwatch carefully.
  • Take repeated readings for accuracy.
  • Use the same volume of liquid for each measurement.
