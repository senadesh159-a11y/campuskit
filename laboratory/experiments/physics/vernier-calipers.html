03. Aim & Apparatus

To measure the diameter of a given object using Vernier Calipers.

Apparatus: Vernier calipers, given object and cleaning cloth.

04. Theory

Vernier calipers are used to measure small lengths accurately. They consist of a main scale and a sliding vernier scale. The instrument has outside jaws, inside jaws and a depth rod. The difference between one main scale division and one vernier scale division gives the least count.

1 Main Scale Division = 1 mm
10 Vernier Scale Divisions = 9 mm
1 Vernier Scale Division = 0.9 mm
Least Count = 1 − 0.9 = 0.1 mm

05. Procedure

  1. Clean the jaws and check the zero position.
  2. Determine the zero error, if any.
  3. Place the object between the outside jaws.
  4. Move the sliding jaw until it gently touches the object.
  5. Note the main scale reading.
  6. Find the coinciding vernier division.
  7. Calculate the observed and corrected readings.
  8. Repeat the experiment and calculate the mean.

07. Result

08. Precautions

  • Clean the jaws before taking measurements.
  • Do not apply excessive pressure on the object.
  • Check zero error before starting.
  • Read the scales without parallax error.
  • Take multiple readings for accuracy.
