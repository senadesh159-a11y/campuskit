Label key

Main scaleFixed graduated scale, usually in cm and mm
Vernier scaleSliding scale used to read a fraction of one main-scale division
Outside jawsMeasure external diameter or thickness
Inside jawsMeasure internal diameter
Depth rodMeasures depth of a hole or vessel
Locking screwHolds the slider in position
KEY RELATIONObserved reading = MSR + (coinciding division × least count) • Corrected reading = observed reading − zero error

Accuracy note Least count and zero correction depend on the actual instrument. Check the instrument's printed least count before recording data.
