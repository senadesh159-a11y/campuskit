Faraday's law

ε = −N (dΦB / dt)

Induced emf is proportional to the rate of change of magnetic flux. Reversing the direction of motion reverses the induced current (Lenz's law).
What to observe

Move the magnet towards and away from the coil. Increase the speed, turns or field strength. Notice the change in induced emf and current direction. The numerical model is an idealised teaching simulation, not a calibrated laboratory instrument.
