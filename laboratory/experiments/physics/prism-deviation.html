GUIDE

Apparatus required

  1. 01Triangular glass prism — refracts the incident beam
  2. 02Ray box / narrow slit with monochromatic source
  3. 03White screen — receives the emergent beam
  4. 04Optical bench with prism table
  5. 05Protractor — measures i, A and e
  6. 06Drawing sheet, pencil and scale

Label key

Incident rayBeam directed towards the first refracting face
Refracted rayBeam travelling inside the glass prism
Emergent rayBeam leaving the second refracting face
Apex angle (A)Angle between the two refracting faces
Deviation (δ)Angle between incident and emergent directions
KEY RELATIONδ = i + e − A • At minimum deviation: μ = sin[(A + δₘ)/2] / sin(A/2)

Accuracy note The ray path is a geometric teaching model. Actual ray alignment and readings must be taken on an optical bench.
