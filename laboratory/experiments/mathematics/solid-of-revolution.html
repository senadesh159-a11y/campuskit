Experiment Controls

Working Process

  1. Choose the radius and height of the rectangle.
  2. Imagine the rectangle rotating about its vertical side.
  3. Use the rotation-angle slider to see the solid form gradually.
  4. Observe that a full 360° rotation produces a cylinder.
  5. Calculate the volume using V = πr²h and compare it with the live result.
