🧮 Solid of Revolution

Rotate a curve around an axis and investigate the volume of the generated 3D solid.

LIVE 3D SIMULATION
Drag to rotate · Scroll to zoom

3D model real-time update hota hai. Mouse/touch se model ko rotate aur zoom karo.

Mathematical Formula

V = πr²h
For a cylinder generated by rotating a rectangle about its side.
Surface area = 2πrh + 2πr²

Experiment Controls

Working Process

  1. Choose the radius and height of the rectangle.
  2. Imagine the rectangle rotating about its vertical side.
  3. Use the rotation-angle slider to see the solid form gradually.
  4. Observe that a full 360° rotation produces a cylinder.
  5. Calculate the volume using V = πr²h and compare it with the live result.

Observation

Change the values and observe how the 3D model responds.
