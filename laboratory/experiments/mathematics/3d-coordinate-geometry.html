Visualise a sphere and a plane, and explore their circle of intersection in three dimensions.

Mathematical Formula

Sphere: x² + y² + z² = r²
Horizontal plane: z = d
Intersection circle radius: ρ = √(r² − d²)

Experiment Controls

Working Process

  1. Start with a sphere centred at the origin.
  2. Move the plane up or down by changing d.
  3. Change the tilt to see a differently oriented plane.
  4. Observe the intersection curve where the plane cuts the sphere.
  5. For a horizontal plane, calculate the circle radius using ρ = √(r² − d²), when |d| ≤ r.

Observation
