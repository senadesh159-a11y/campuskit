CampusKit Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

CampusKit is a student-focused application designed to help students manage assignments, class timetables, notes and study activities. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use CampusKit.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, CampusKit may process your Google account information for sign-in, and information you enter such as assignments, timetable entries and notes. If you upload attachments or use other features, the information required for those features may also be processed.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide CampusKit features, save your entries, support sign-in, and improve the app's functionality and reliability.

3. Google Services

CampusKit may use Google services for authentication and data storage. If Google Drive integration is enabled, the app may request permission to access files according to the permissions shown during Google's authorization process. You can choose whether to grant those permissions.

Google's handling of information is also subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Storage and Security

Information may be stored using the services configured for CampusKit. Reasonable steps are taken to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Sharing of Information

CampusKit does not intend to sell personal information. Information may be processed by the third-party services needed to operate features you use, according to their applicable terms and privacy policies.

6. Your Choices

You can choose what information you enter and whether to grant optional permissions. You may stop using CampusKit at any time. For assistance with privacy-related questions or data deletion requests, contact the developer using the email address provided in the app's Google authorization information.

7. Children's Privacy

CampusKit is intended for students. Do not submit information you are not authorized to share. If you believe personal information has been submitted improperly, contact the developer.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as CampusKit's features change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
