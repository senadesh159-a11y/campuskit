      CAMPUSKIT • LEARNING INTELLIGENCE

      Student Intelligence Lab

      Five experimental learning tools: track unfinished concepts, investigate readings, challenge explanations, map doubt patterns and learn backwards from answers.

      🧩 Concept Debt Tracker

      Track concepts you have postponed or only partly understood.

      🧪 Lab Mistake Detective

      Enter at least three readings. Compare the median, spread and possible outliers before checking your lab notebook and procedure.

      Your reading analysis will appear here.

      🥊 Opponent Teacher

      Become your own strict examiner. Get counter-questions that expose assumptions, missing evidence and weak explanations.

      🧬 Doubt DNA

      Save doubts, see your strongest confusion pattern and remove doubts once resolved.

      🔁 Reverse Learning

      Begin with the final answer, then reconstruct the question, assumptions, method and verification.

      Privacy: tools use browser local storage on this device. They do not upload your study data. Lab outlier checks are statistical hints, not validation of experimental correctness.
